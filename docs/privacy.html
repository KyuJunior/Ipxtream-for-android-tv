Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Core Principle: IPXtream TV does not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal data. Everything runs locally on your television device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe private viewing should stay private. IPXtream TV does not gather:

  • Personal identity details (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
  • Viewing history, watch time, or channel selections.
  • Device location data or GPS coordinates.
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking identifiers.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To function properly as an IPTV player, the app saves certain configuration items locally in private Android storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences and Room Database):

  • Provider Credentials: The server URL, username, and password you provide to access your IPTV service.
  • Playlists and EPG Data: Downloaded category lists, channel indexes, and electronic program guide caches to enable fast local search and browsing.
  • Favorites and Playback History: Channels or movies you mark as favorites, along with resume positions for video playback.
  • Application Preferences: Player buffer sizes, stream format preferences, and UI settings.

This data never leaves your device. It is stored strictly within the private sandboxed storage of the application and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data in Android settings.

3. Network Connections and Outgoing Requests

The application initiates outgoing network connections in only two situations:

  • Connecting to Your IPTV Provider: When loading playlists, EPG schedules, or video streams, the app communicates directly with the server address you entered. We do not proxy, inspect, or intercept these connections.
  • Checking for Application Updates: To notify you of new software releases, the app queries the public GitHub Releases API. This request only fetches public version metadata and transmits no personal information.

4. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

IPXtream TV does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or telemetry packages (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Facebook SDK).

5. Children's Privacy

The application does not collect information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Parental controls can be set directly within the player settings to restrict access to specific categories.

6. Security of Your Data

Because your credentials and stream data remain exclusively on your local hardware, their security depends on the security of your local Android TV device and network. We recommend using secure Wi-Fi networks and strong provider passwords.

7. Contact and Inquiries

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue or discussion on the official GitHub repository at github.com/KyuJunior/Ipxtream-for-android-tv.

IPXtream TV Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Applies to IPXtream TV for Android TV (Google Play and GitHub versions)

IPXtream TV is a video player for an IPTV service you already have. It does not include, sell or recommend any channels, films or subscriptions. The developer runs no server for the app and receives no data from it.

What stays on your TV

  • The provider address, username and password you enter (stored encrypted).
  • Favourites, watch history and playback positions, settings, the channel you chose for a match.
  • Copies of your provider's catalogue, kept to make the app faster.
  • A short log of the last app crashes, shown in Settings, with logins masked. It is never sent anywhere.

All of it is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The app does not use backups or analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

What the app sends, and to whom

RecipientWhat and why
Your IPTV provider (the address you enter)Your username and password, the lists of channels, films and series, and the streams you play. Many providers only offer unencrypted HTTP; the app tells you so on the sign-in screen and uses HTTPS when a new provider offers it. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.
Image servers named by your providerPosters and channel logos from your provider's catalogue.
ESPN (site.api.espn.com and its image servers)Football fixtures, scores and team crests for the Matches section. No account details are sent.
Google PlayInstalling and updating the app (Google Play version).
GitHubChecking for and downloading updates (GitHub version only).
beIN's public TV guideFinding which beIN channel shows a match (GitHub version only). No account details are sent.

Like any internet request, these services can see your TV's IP address. The developer does not receive, sell or share any of this data.

Children

The app is not designed for children. What it plays depends entirely on the service you connect.

Your choices

You can remove an account in Settings → Accounts Manager, clear the crash log in Settings → About, or delete everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete on our side.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: contact@dryaser.me

سياسة الخصوصية لتطبيق IPXtream TV

سارية من 24 أكتوبر 2026 · تنطبق على تطبيق IPXtream TV لأجهزة Android TV (نسخة Google Play ونسخة GitHub)

IPXtream TV مشغّل فيديو لخدمة IPTV لديك بالفعل. لا يتضمن التطبيق أي قنوات أو أفلام أو اشتراكات، ولا يبيعها ولا يرشّحها. لا يشغّل المطوّر أي خادم للتطبيق ولا يستلم منه أي بيانات.

ما يبقى على تلفازك

  • عنوان المزوّد واسم المستخدم وكلمة المرور التي تدخلها (مخزّنة بشكل مشفّر).
  • المفضلة وسجل المشاهدة ومواضع التشغيل والإعدادات والقناة التي اخترتها لكل مباراة.
  • نسخ من قوائم المزوّد لتسريع التطبيق.
  • سجل قصير لآخر أعطال التطبيق يظهر في الإعدادات مع إخفاء بيانات الدخول، ولا يُرسل إلى أي جهة.

يُحذف كل ذلك عند مسح بيانات التطبيق أو إلغاء تثبيته. لا يستخدم التطبيق النسخ الاحتياطي ولا أدوات التحليلات أو الإعلانات أو التتبّع.

How to Install

Get running on your television in four direct steps.

Step 1

Get the APK File

Download the latest release APK from the GitHub releases page on your computer or directly on your TV device.

Step 2

Enable Unknown Sources

In your Android TV Settings, navigate to Security and Restrictions, then enable permission for your file manager or Downloader app.

Step 3

Install the Application

Open the APK with your TV package installer, confirm permissions, and let the installation complete.

Step 4

Connect Your Provider

Launch IPXtream TV from your app drawer. Enter your Xtream Codes server URL, username, and password to load your content.
